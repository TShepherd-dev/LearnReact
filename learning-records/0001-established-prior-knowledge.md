# Established prior knowledge: SPA/Vue2/.NET senior background

Tim comes in as a 15+ year .NET backend developer with deep hands-on Vue2 + Vuex + vue-router + vue-i18n frontend experience on a large production SPA (an internal app). He is NOT experienced with React.

This is the floor for the zone of proximal development: he already understands components, reactive state, routing, permissions-gated navigation, breadcrumbs, i18n, module-based stores, and async data flow abstractly. Future sessions should NOT re-teach those web/SPA fundamentals; they should teach React's specific idioms and the Vue→React mapping that leverages what he already knows.

## Evidence
Self-reported in the opening session, plus a review of the source Vue2 codebase (Vuex 3 store with 39 modules, vue-router 3 with rich route meta, vue-i18n, axios, Vite) that confirms the claimed depth.

## Implications
- Every lesson should be built around translating a real, named source Vue file into idiomatic React, so his existing mental model maps cleanly onto React concepts.
- Resume/interview vocabulary matters (TypeScript, hooks, etc.) — keep the toolchain relevant to the Australian market.
